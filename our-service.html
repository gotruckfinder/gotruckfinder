PROFESSIONAL VEHICLE INSPECTION

Understand the Vehicle Before You Buy

Our technical checks focus on key vehicle systems and the condition of the installed equipment. The scope of inspection and available records can be discussed for each vehicle.

01

Appearance & Structure

Body condition, visible damage, signs of repair and structural areas.

02

Engine & Powertrain

Engine operation, visible leaks, abnormal noise and relevant powertrain condition.

03

Transmission & Drivetrain

Transmission operation, clutch where applicable, axles and drivetrain components.

04

Working Equipment

Crane, lifting system, tank equipment or other installed machinery, as applicable.

05

Chassis & Braking

Chassis, suspension, tyres, axles and braking system condition.

06

Inspection Feedback

Available findings, photos and videos to help buyers review the vehicle remotely.

Inspection feedback reflects the checks performed and information available for each vehicle. Independent third-party inspection can be coordinated upon request and subject to availability.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions from International Buyers

What types of vehicles can I source through GoTruckFinder?

Our main categories include truck cranes, aerial work platforms, sanitation trucks and tank trucks. Contact us if you need a specific model or configuration.

Can you help me find a vehicle that is not listed on the website?

Yes. Share the model, specifications, budget and destination country. We can check our available vehicle resources and partner channels.

We aim to provide available vehicle photos, videos and condition information for your review before you decide whether to proceed.

Third-party inspection can be coordinated upon request, subject to the vehicle, location and inspection provider’s availability. Any inspection fee and scope should be confirmed in advance.

Transit time depends on the destination, shipping route, vessel schedule, shipping method and port conditions. We can help check the available arrangements for your destination.

Can you assist with export documents and shipping?

We can assist with export-related documentation and coordinate shipping arrangements with relevant service providers, according to the transaction scope.
