01 WHO WE ARE

Connecting global buyers with China's used commercial vehicle market.

Established in 2022, GoTruckFinder was created to help international buyers source reliable used trucks and special-purpose vehicles from China with greater transparency and confidence.

GoTruckFinder operates as an independent business brand. We cooperate with experienced companies and vehicle-industry partners in China, including Chengli Special Automobile Co., Ltd. Our partners' experience and industry resources complement our focus on the used commercial vehicle market.
