01   /   WHO WE ARE

A direct connection to China's used commercial vehicle market.

GoTruckFinder was established in 2022 with a clear purpose: to help overseas buyers source used trucks and special-purpose vehicles from China with greater transparency and confidence.

We operate as an independent business brand, working with vehicle owners, sourcing channels and experienced industry partners. We focus on understanding each buyer's application, budget and destination requirements, then presenting suitable vehicle options with the information available.

02   /   OUR INDUSTRY CONNECTION

Independent in operation. Connected to industry experience.

Our work is supported by cooperation with established companies and professionals in China's special-purpose vehicle industry, including Chengli Special Automobile Co., Ltd.

Chengli's background in special-purpose vehicle manufacturing provides relevant industry context and a broader network of vehicle knowledge. GoTruckFinder, however, operates as an independent brand focused on used commercial vehicle sourcing for international buyers.
